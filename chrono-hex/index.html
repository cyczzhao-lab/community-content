          Rules in One Paragraph:


          Chrono-Hex: Tempo Nexus is a 2–4 player turn-based tactical strategy game on a 37-hex board where each commander starts at an outer Citadel with 4 units and 4 Energy (⚡), spending 2 Action Points (AP) per turn across four non-dominant actions: MARCH (0⚡, move units to reinforce or attack an adjacent hex, capturing it if attacking units strictly exceed defender units plus +1 Aegis Shield), DEPLOY (2⚡, +2 units on any owned hex), FORTIFY (2⚡, +1 unit and an Aegis Shield on an owned hex), or EMP PULSE (4⚡, range-2 orbital strike removing 2 enemy units and stripping shields). At the end of each turn, players harvest Energy (+2 base, +1 per Citadel, +2 per captured Energy Relay, +1 for the central Tempo Nexus) and Victory Points (+1 per Citadel, +3 for the central Tempo Nexus, and +1 per 3 owned hexes); the first player to reach 25 VP, conquer all Citadels, or lead in VP after 12 rounds wins.
        


        

          Why No Single Dominant Strategy Exists:
          
    
            
  • Rush (Aggressor) races for the central Tempo Nexus (+3 VP/turn), but central defenders get surrounded and hammered by range-2 EMP Pulses.
    • 
            
  • Turtle (Fortifier) stacks Aegis Shields on chokepoints, but loses the Energy economy if rivals capture the outer Energy Relays (+2⚡/turn).
    • 
            
  • Greed (Harvester) spreads thin across Relays, leaving its home Citadel vulnerable to a concentrated March.
